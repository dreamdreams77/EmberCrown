# LPC art credits (Ashfall Keep art trial)

The files in `assets/lpc/` are derived from Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) artwork. They are **not** covered by the game's MIT licence: they keep their original licences listed below. Our changes (recolouring and compositing into sprite sheets, cropping and recolouring two tiles) are released under the same terms (CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0, and OGA-BY 3.0 or CC0 where the source allows).

- `assets/lpc/shiloh.png`, `assets/lpc/guard_marrow.png`: composited from the Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator, https://github.com/liberatedpixelcup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator (layers listed below).
- `assets/lpc/ashfall_tiles.png`: castle floor and castle wall tiles by Daniel Armstrong (HughSpectrum), CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0, via the LPC Starter Kit, https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/RPG_Maker_MZ_LPC_Starter_Kit (see its "Final Attribution" file).
